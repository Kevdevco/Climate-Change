Will climate change cause societal collapse?

Lacey on tipping points

Sudden collapse?

Possible, but the timing and scale are uncertain.

Lenton et al. (2019); Kemp et al. (2022)

Emmanuel on food security

Harvest failure?

A real risk if major breadbaskets fail together.

Myers et al. (2014); Cottrell et al. (2019); Gaupp et al. (2020)

Kevin on conflict and migration

War and displacement?

Climate raises the risk; existing conditions decide the outcome.

Mach et al. (2019); Watson et al. (2023); Hoffmann et al. (2024)

Stella on government action

Can policy prevent it?

It can cut the risk, but pledges still outrun action.

Roelfsema et al. (2020); Falkner (2016); UNEP (2023)

Kewe on the economy and finance

Financial contagion?

Climate shocks can spread across the whole financial system.

Bolton et al. (2020); Battiston et al. (2017); Curcio et al. (2023)

Mark on Ireland

What about Ireland?

Farming is exposed, and the costs reach the wider economy.

Desmond et al. (2017); Sweeney et al. (2008); de Bruin et al. (2024); Mozahid & Gardebroek (2025)

Our answer

Not inevitable, but climate change multiplies existing risks.
